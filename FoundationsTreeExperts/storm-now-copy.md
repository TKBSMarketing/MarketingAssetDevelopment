# STORM NOW — 12 Simple Variations

Built 2026-09-04 during an active storm. Deliberately stripped back: hook question,
action line, phone number. Nothing else competing for the two seconds someone gives an
ad while their driveway is blocked.

**Phone on every creative:** 1-833-TREE-365
**CTA Button:** Call Now
**Creative:** `ads/storm-now/` — each variation at 4:5 (feed) and 9:16 (Reels/Stories)

Primary text is short on purpose. This reader has a tree on their house, is scanning on a
phone, and may be on battery power. Do not swap in the longform always-on copy.

---

## Launch checklist

1. Campaign 3 (Storm Response) on. Daily budget **$25–30**, pulled from Campaign 1.
2. **CTA button: Call Now.** Not Get Quote.
3. Pause the always-on emergency ads (E1–E4) so they don't bid against these.
4. Tighten the radius to the actual hit area if the storm was localized.
5. Start with 4–5 of the 12, not all twelve — at this budget Meta needs a small set to find
   a winner. Add the rest as replacements when one fatigues.
6. **Day 5–7: turn it all off.** Storm intent decays fast.

## DYNAMIC TEXT — use this

One pool, mixed by Meta across all 12 creatives. Three of each — the spread covers
reassurance, symptom-naming, and the competitive angle, without three lines that say
the same thing in different words.

Every line here is deliberately generic. The creatives already carry the specific hook
("Tree on your car?", "Driveway blocked?"), so the copy must not name an object — otherwise
Meta will eventually pair "tree on your car" text with the blocked-driveway image and the
ad contradicts itself. The per-ad copy further down is kept only as reference for what each
image says; don't paste it into a dynamic ad set.

**CTA button:** Call Now

### Primary Text (3)

**PT1**
Storm damage in Ann Arbor? We answer 24/7.

Tree down, limb on the roof, something leaning that wasn't leaning yesterday — call 1-833-TREE-365.

Licensed and insured. Not a storm chaser passing through.

**PT2**
Storm damage? We're here to help.

Fallen trees, broken limbs, or anything threatening your property — Foundations Tree Experts is available 24/7 for emergency tree service and storm cleanup.

Call 1-833-TREE-365. We're ready when you need us.

**PT3**
Everyone's calling a tree company today. Most of them are being told "next week."

We're already out. Emergency removals get triaged first — hazards over houses, driveways, and power lines before anything cosmetic.

1-833-TREE-365. 24/7, real person, no callback queue.

### Headlines (3)
- Emergency Tree Removal — 24/7
- Storm Damage? Call Now.
- Crews Out Now — Ann Arbor

### Descriptions (3)
- 24/7 · 1-833-TREE-365
- Crews Out Now · Ann Arbor
- Licensed · Insured · Local

All headlines are under Meta's 40-character display limit and all descriptions under 30,
so nothing truncates mid-phrase.
